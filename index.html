Know the risks

A few simple habits can help keep your personal information safer online.

🎣

Spot phishing

Be careful with urgent messages, unexpected links, and requests for passwords or OTPs. Verify the sender through a trusted channel.

🔐

Use strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts. A password manager can help you keep track of them.

📲

Protect your OTP

Never share an OTP, PIN, or recovery code with anyone. Genuine support staff should not ask you to reveal these secrets.

🌐

Browse safely

Check website addresses carefully, keep software updated, and avoid entering sensitive details on unfamiliar sites.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Protect your privacy

Think before posting personal details, live location, school information, or private photos publicly.

💳

Avoid online scams

Be suspicious of guaranteed prizes, too-good-to-be-true offers, and pressure to pay quickly. Verify before acting.

Remember: Stop, verify, and ask a trusted adult or official support channel if a message feels suspicious.
